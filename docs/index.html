Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Loopwave does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Video files you choose are read directly from your device to preview and set them as a wallpaper. They are never uploaded, copied elsewhere, or shared with any third party.

Loopwave does not use the internet. It has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

The only information kept is a reference to which video you last chose, along with your speed and scale preferences — stored locally on your device and never transmitted anywhere.

If a future version of this app adds a feature that uses the internet (for example, advertising), this policy will be updated first, and the change will be reflected here and on the Play Store listing.
